Purpose: one brand for Google Business Profile, Instagram and WhatsApp marketing automation and business AI. These are researched candidates, not a rebrand or a domain purchase.

My preference for understandable meaning is **SociLoom**. “Your business. Every channel. In sync.” is a possible positioning line; AI and automation can be explained in the descriptor rather than making the brand Instagram-specific.

## What was actually checked

- Exact quoted-name searches and additional marketing/AI/software queries, plus selected spaced/phonetic variants.
- The .in registry's authoritative RDAP endpoint, discovered through [IANA's RDAP bootstrap](https://data.iana.org/rdap/dns.json).
- [sociloom.in registry lookup](https://rdap.nixiregistry.in/rdap/domain/sociloom.in), [sociqen.in registry lookup](https://rdap.nixiregistry.in/rdap/domain/sociqen.in), [marklynt.in registry lookup](https://rdap.nixiregistry.in/rdap/domain/marklynt.in).
- Positive control: the same service returned HTTP200 and the domain name for the registered `google.in`, so an unavailable lookup service was not mistaken for three available domains.

HTTP404 means no domain registration record was returned at check time. It does not prove a domain is purchasable: registry reservations, restrictions, premium pricing and changes after this check can affect a registrar's final offer. Check availability and price at checkout before committing to a name.

No internet search can establish that nobody anywhere uses a word. Search engines omit unindexed businesses, private accounts, new products and some regional records. No trademark clearance, company-registry clearance or social-handle reservation was completed. The [IP India trademark search](https://ipindia.gov.in/trade-marks-before-you-apply-search-existing-trademarks) links to official search tools; the public portal requires interactive verification. Exact and similar names need checking for the intended goods/services before launch. Domain availability and trademark rights are separate questions.
